# 7 Iconic Lauren Conrad Street Style Outfits & How to Recreate Them

There are tons of amazing **lauren conrad street style** looks that continue to inspire fashion lovers everywhere. As a true fashion mogul, LC always looks so put-together while rocking the latest trends. Whether she's sharing her favorite picks on her blog or launching new collections, she helps us all stay in the loop. She's come so far in the fashion world, looking just as fabulous walking down the street as she does at any red carpet event. Check out some of my favorite **lauren conrad outfits** and learn how to master her signature style!

## 1. Spring Chic

![Spring Chic](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/o7/mp/cfuu5mmr_550x825.jpg)

Lauren looks effortlessly chic no matter what. For spring, she's all about loose-fitting blouses tucked into shorts, and of course, some wedges to elongate the legs! This is a look that can easily be recreated with clothes from [Forever 21](https://www.forever21.com/). It's cheap and easy, and you'll be earning major style points like LC! This is just one of the awesome street style looks from Lauren Conrad.

## 2. Print + Jeans

![Print + Jeans](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/tp/bh/0q4pvkjp_468x750.jpg)

Whenever you don't know what to wear, grab some skinnies and a shirt with a fun print on it. You can never go wrong with that. Lauren keeps her street style really casual whenever she's just running errands. Black skinnies and a cheetah-print top, paired with oversized sunglasses and a high bun, will make the perfect go-to outfit.

**Lauren's Street Style Essentials:**

- Oversized sunglasses for an instant glam look

- High-quality denim in various washes

- Neutral-toned flats and comfortable wedges

- Vibrant accessories for a pop of color

## 3. Denim on Denim

![Denim on Denim](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/qr/j2/rdo9nobv_350x583.jpg)

This spring, try the whole denim on denim look! Lauren rocks this look from time to time and looks fab. You can rock dark-wash skinnies with a light chambray shirt, or just rock a denim jacket over one of your favorite tees. It might sound like too much, but denim on denim always looks great. For shoes, go with something casual like converse or combat boots!

## 4. Business Chic

![Business Chic](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ll/x6/dwb01gbq_600x857.jpg)

I love that Lauren always looks super classy and business-ready. It's actually such an easy look to achieve. For a business casual look, rock some dark skinnies with a tucked-in button up shirt. Throw a [blazer](https://allwomenstalk.com/ways-to-wear-a-blazer/) over it and bam! All done. For shoes, I love nude-colored flats. I think they give a touch of sophistication.

## 5. Midi Chic

![Midi Chic](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/d7/m5/otr6xa56_433x650.jpg)

I've been seeing midi skirts everywhere and I am so in love with this trend. Lauren rocks it with a really simple outfit that's super easy to recreate. Personally, I am a huge fan of navy blue paired with white. So, rocking a white tank tucked into a navy blue midi skirt sounds amazing! You can find some adorable midi skirts at [ASOS](https://www.asos.com/). If you need more inspiration, check out these [tips for wearing midi skirts](https://allwomenstalk.com/tips-for-wearing-midi-skirts/).

## 6. Floral

![Floral](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ay/h0/h30mfefn_395x594.jpg)

You can never go wrong with floral. It's the perfect look to recreate for spring! You can find tons of adorable and affordable floral dresses on [Forever 21](http://www.forever21.com). I love dresses with huge flowers - it's a fun way to make a fashion statement. Much like her [Spring Chic](#{1}) look, this is all about embracing the season.

## 7. Pop of Color

![Pop of Color](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/y8/ki/qok95x8i_553x657.jpg)

I love keeping an outfit really neutral and then adding a pop of color. An easy way to do this to rock a black tee with some vibrant colored high-waisted shorts. You can opt for a bright orange or even swap them for more pastel-colored ones that are perfect for spring!

Well, there you have it! These are just seven out of many awesome street style looks from Lauren Conrad. She's become quite the fashionista and I love keeping up with her latest looks. If you follow her Instagram ([@laurenconrad](https://www.instagram.com/laurenconrad/)), you'll see more fashion-related pictures. You can also check out her official collection, [LC Lauren Conrad](https://www.kohls.com/catalog/lc-lauren-conrad.jsp?CN=Brand:LC%20Lauren%20Conrad), for more inspiration. Of the list, which look is your favorite? Which one will you be trying to recreate?

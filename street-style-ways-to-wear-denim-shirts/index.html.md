# 6 Easy Street Style Ways to Wear a Blue Shirt

A blue button-down works with more than denim. These six outfits take it from casual weekends and city errands to the office, summer outings, and date night.

## 1. Weekend Denim

![Weekend Denim blue shirt outfit](https://img.allw.mn/uploads/01-weekend-denim-1790776180854.png)

Try a blue striped button-down with denim shorts, a straw hat, and simple sandals for an easy weekend outfit.

## 2. City Casual

![City Casual blue shirt outfit](https://img.allw.mn/uploads/02-city-casual-1790776181857.png)

Tuck a light blue shirt into straight-leg jeans, then add white sneakers and a structured crossbody bag.

## 3. Office Polish

![Office Polish blue shirt outfit](https://img.allw.mn/uploads/03-office-polish-1790776182891.png)

Pair a blue button-down with tailored beige trousers, loafers, and a leather tote for a polished work look.

## 4. Summer Light

![Summer Light blue shirt outfit](https://img.allw.mn/uploads/04-summer-light-1790776183613.png)

Wear a breezy striped shirt with white linen pants, woven accessories, and flat sandals on warm days.

## 5. Date Night

![Date Night blue shirt outfit](https://img.allw.mn/uploads/05-date-night-1790776184453.png)

Dress up a blue shirt with a satin midi skirt, delicate gold jewelry, and strappy heels.

## 6. Layered Cool

![Layered Cool blue shirt outfit](https://img.allw.mn/uploads/06-layered-cool-1790776185261.png)

Leave a blue shirt open over a white tank, then finish with relaxed trousers and clean white sneakers.

# 7 Miranda Kerr Street Style Looks You Can Easily Recreate

There are so many fabulous looks from Miranda Kerr that are worth recreating. This supermodel knows how to look stylish even when she's not strutting the runway. I absolutely love her fashion sense: it's practical, effortless, and she constantly earns major style points. Whether you are looking for chic city attire or elevated everyday outfit ideas, off-duty model fashion offers endless inspiration. Check out some of my favorite Miranda Kerr street style looks below and let me know which Miranda Kerr outfit you'll be trying out next!

## 1. Power Woman

![Power Woman](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/u2/ed/ytrycrj9_409x686.jpg)

When doesn't Miranda Kerr look like a million bucks? Even when she's just running errands in the city, she looks like a power woman. I'm loving everything about this black and grey look! To recreate it, pair your mini dress with some incredible leather knee-high boots. Throw a black trench coat over it (you can find timeless outerwear options at [Burberry](https://www.burberry.com)) and make sure you remember your oversized purse! With this outfit, you'll look like you're ready to take over the world. This is just one of the gorgeous street style looks from Miranda Kerr that demonstrates how structured tailoring transforms an everyday ensemble.

- Fitted mini dress or sleek base layer
- Tailored black trench coat
- Leather knee-high boots
- Oversized structured tote bag

## 2. Menswear Inspired

![Menswear Inspired](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/hs/wj/isrkk9w7_467x700.jpg)

I love when women put a "menswear" twist to their outfit. The white collared shirt and tie underneath are very business-ready. The structured blazer makes the outfit look very tailored and refined. To recreate this look, rock a white button up shirt and pair it with a crisp tie on top! Wear a mini skirt on top of your black stockings and then throw on your favorite menswear-inspired Miranda Kerr blazer style to tie the whole outfit together.

## 3. Fun Prints

![Fun Prints](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/dm/zj/b609ok8g_450x750.jpg)

When she's not glammed up, Miranda keeps things casual. I love everything about this look! It's simple yet makes a statement at the same time. To recreate this look, go for some dark skinnies and pair them with your favorite black flats. Keep your tee and purse black too. That way, you can wear a fun printed cardigan without making the outfit look too overwhelming. For the ultimate casual Miranda Kerr street style look, wear your hair up in a topknot bun! If you prefer a more tailored silhouette like the [menswear inspired](#2) outfit, this relaxed printed layer provides the perfect casual alternative.

| Outfit Style | Key Piece | Highlight Accessory |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Power Woman | Black Trench Coat | Oversized Tote |
| Fun Prints | Patterned Cardigan | Topknot Hair Bun |
| All Black | Oversized Knit | Dark Sunglasses |

## 4. All Black Ensemble

![All Black Ensemble](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/jl/wh/hw9ydgo3_598x1024.jpg)

You can never go wrong with an all black ensemble. It always looks sleek, monochrome, and sophisticated. Miranda rocks all-black all the time! I love the oversized sweater over the dark skinnies. The hat and classic shades complete the look, along with that purse! Similar to the confidence of the [power woman](#1) aesthetic, a monochrome black palette instantly creates a polished look. Pick up your favorite designer shades at [Ray-Ban](https://www.ray-ban.com) to complete the outfit.

## 5. Denim on Denim

![Denim on Denim](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fq/z3/nqoth071_600x901.jpg)

Who knew denim on denim could look so good? Miranda pulls it off flawlessly! If you think you can't pull off the whole denim on denim look, think again. Denim looks great on everyone! Pair your distressed denim cutoffs with your denim button-up shirt. Find a colorful purse and you're ready to go! You can grab quality denim pieces from classic brands like [Levi's](https://www.levi.com) to build your double-denim foundation.

- Distressed denim shorts or slim jeans
- Classic button-down denim shirt
- Vibrant pop-of-color crossbody purse

## 6. Preppy Casual

![Preppy Casual](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ky/hy/cwstztok_552x1180.jpg)

I am obsessed with this look. The white collared tee and black skirt are very preppy. The denim jacket, boots, and hat make this look more casual. It's the perfect outfit for running errands around the city, meeting your girlfriends for lunch, or even heading to class! If you love double denim like the [denim on denim](#5) outfit, pairing a denim jacket over a preppy skirt gives you a lighter, layered look. P.S. how awesome is that purse? I love how it matches the hat!

## 7. Leather

![Leather](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/mo/p9/ziv9jt2j_500x709.jpg)

Leather is always in. I love this look because of the simplicity of it. To recreate this look, just pair your leather shorts with a simple white tee. To add a pop of color to the outfit, choose some vibrant colored flats. I love the bold red lip color she has on: it actually helps take the outfit to the next level! Complete the beauty look with an iconic red lipstick from [Sephora](https://www.sephora.com) for that effortless model-off-duty touch.

Well, there you have it! These are just seven out of many awesome street style looks from Miranda Kerr. Among all these iconic outfits, which Miranda Kerr best looks are your personal favorite?
